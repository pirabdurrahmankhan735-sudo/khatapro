# Privacy Policy – KhataPro (draft for Phase 1)

Last updated: 2026-11-15

KhataPro is an offline business ledger. This draft describes the app as it is in Phase 1.
Update it whenever a later phase adds features (backup, cloud, analytics, etc.) and before publishing.

**Data stored:** customer names, mobile numbers, addresses, notes and transactions that you enter.
**Where:** only in the app's private storage on your own device. The app has no internet access
code and requests no Android permissions.
**Sharing:** none. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs that collect data.
**Backup:** Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for this app's data in Phase 1.
Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all records permanently.
**Your responsibility:** you are the owner of your customers' information. Keep your phone locked.
